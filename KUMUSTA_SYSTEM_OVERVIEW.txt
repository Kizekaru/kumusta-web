1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW: CIVIC ACCESS & OPPORTUNITY PLATFORM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KUMUSTA is a civic-tech system designed under the 
"Civic Access & Opportunity Platforms" framework. It democratizes mental health 
intervention and safeguards the academic and socio-economic future of students across the
province of Pampanga.

In the Philippines, public school students face a critical barrier to civic 
equity: a severe shortage of guidance counselors (often 1 counselor to over 
1,000 students) and inaccessible private psychological care. Left unchecked, 
crises like severe depression, bullying, self-harm, and acute distress lead to 
school dropouts, diminished potential, and compromised life outcomes.

As a Civic Access & Opportunity Platform, KUMUSTA bridges this systemic gap by:
* Universal Civic Access: Providing every public school student an instant, 
  cost-free, and stigma-free digital channel for early crisis intervention.
* Opportunity Preservation: Preventing dropouts and academic derailment by 
  intervening early, ensuring vulnerable youth stay in school and achieve their 
  full future potential.
* Civic-Municipal Synergy: Linking DepEd campus counselors with Municipal Local 
  Government Units (LGUs) and Rural Health Units (RHUs) for synchronized, 
  data-driven public service delivery.
* Privacy as a Civic Right: 100% Zero-PII isolation compliant with Republic 
  Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and DepEd Child Protection Policies, 
  guaranteeing that seeking help never jeopardizes student dignity or rights.


2. CORE FEATURES (CIVIC ACCESS & OPPORTUNITY LENS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1] Equal-Access Predictive Crisis Triage
    * Real-time telemetry that ingests and evaluates student distress signals 
      in under 450ms, categorizing risks into LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, and CRITICAL.
    * Civic Value: Eliminates queues and socioeconomic bias—every student in 
      need is evaluated with equal priority and sub-180 second triage response.

[2] Counselor Empowerment & Clinical Briefing Engine
    * Delivers instant clinical briefs to overburdened public school guidance 
      counselors, complete with evidence-based "Do Say" (de-escalating, empathic) 
      and "Don't Say" (anti-stigmatizing) protocols.
    * Opportunity Value: Multiplies counselor capacity, allowing single-person 
      guidance offices to handle high-risk situations effectively and keep students 
      engaged in their education.

[3] Municipal Civic Heatmap & Resource Telemetry
    * Interactive geospatial map of Pampanga (San Fernando, Angeles City, 
      Guagua, Lubao, Mabalacat, Mexico, etc.) mapping aggregated incident densities.
    * Civic Value: Transforms municipal governance from guesswork to precision 
      public service. LGUs can deploy mental health professionals, subsidies, 
      and youth community programs directly to underserved barangays and schools.

[4] Inter-Agency Civic Coordination Mesh (Dual Persona)
    * Connects grassroots school teams (School Principals & Guidance Staff) with 
      municipal decision-makers (City Health Officers & LGU Risk Coordinators).
    * Opportunity Value: Breaks institutional silos so schools are never left 
      alone to manage crises without municipal LGU backing and public health resources.

[5] Case Lifecycle Management & Policy Audit Reporting
    * Transparent workflow tracking incidents from OPEN -> IN PROGRESS -> RESOLVED 
      with complete accountability.
    * Civic Value: Generates one-click PDF audit documentation for Sangguniang 
      Bayan/Panlungsod policy formulation, ensuring local youth mental health 
      budgets are backed by empirical campus data.

[6] Zero-PII Civic Isolation & Cultural Safety
    * Guaranteed student anonymity through cryptographic session isolation.
    * Civic Value: Replaces institutional fear and cultural stigma with safe, 
      uncompromised civic access, tailored to Filipino and Kapampangan family dynamics.
